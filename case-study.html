[ 03 · WHAT WE’RE WORKING ON ]

Helping more people in Rwanda get to know Hope Haven and its impact.

There is a lot happening at Hope Haven, from what students are learning today to what graduates do next. Our job is to find those stories, tell them clearly and make sure they reach the people who should see them.

A central priority is building awareness in Rwanda: showing the school’s work and the talent, ideas and achievements of students from different community backgrounds. We also film and photograph on the ground for Hope Haven’s US team, giving them material to use in their own communications.

[ 04 · HOW WE WORK ]

Start with the people. Find the story. Make the work.

We listen to students, teachers and staff, and work with the team to understand what each story says about Hope Haven as a whole. Then we decide how best to tell it: a short film, a photo series, a social post or a printed publication.

The work continues throughout the year, with regular content alongside coverage of the school’s major moments.
