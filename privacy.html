Privacy and Cookie Notice

This notice describes the Qrate website and where to ask about information or images you have shared with us.

When you contact us

The project form asks for your name, email address and project note. You may also provide your organisation and the type of work you need. Submitting the form sends those details through EmailJS so Qrate can respond.

Booking a call opens Calendly on a separate website. You can email Qrate directly if you prefer.

Email Qrate Calendly privacy notice

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are data a website can ask your browser to store and send back on later requests. Local storage saves a value in your browser for the website to read. Qrate uses local storage to remember whether to allow embedded YouTube players.

Videos are off on your first visit. If you allow them, pages containing a film can load a YouTube player. You can switch them off again here. Your choice has no set expiry in the site code. Clearing this site’s browser data resets it.

You can read the resource guides without registering or entering contact details.

Services used by the website

Hosting and display

GitHub Pages serves the site. For page layout, type and icons, your browser also requests files from Tailwind CSS, Google Fonts and unpkg. The contact page loads the EmailJS library from jsDelivr.

Films, publications and booking

Allowing embedded videos connects your browser to YouTube. Opening the magazine, booking calendar or a social link takes you to that provider’s website, where its own privacy notice applies.

Field photography and filming

Questions about an interview, photograph or film may relate to an agreement with the organisation that commissioned it. Send the project name, a link or a description so we can identify the material and discuss the request with you.

When planning work involving children, discuss guardian permission, student participation, supervision and where the material may appear with the school or commissioning partner.

Privacy and safeguarding enquiries

To ask about your personal information, request a correction or removal, or raise a concern about published material, contact Qrate in Kigali, Rwanda.

comms@qrate.rw

Cookie preferences

Embedded videos are optional. Allowing them loads YouTube on pages with a film. You can also watch on YouTube without enabling a player here.
