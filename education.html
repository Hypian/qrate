Child Safeguarding Code

Zero compromises on child protection and student dignity.

In educational communications, safety and ethics precede aesthetics. Every member of Qrate's media team adheres to strict child safeguarding protocols compliant with Rwandan law (Law N° 71/2018) and international safeguarding standards.

01. Parental Consent Registries

Written and verified parental or guardian consent is obtained and logged before any lens is pointed toward a student.

02. Dignified Agency

We reject "poverty tourism" and pity imagery. Students are depicted as capable scholars, leaders, artists, and creators.

03. Chaperoned Campus Crews

Qrate crew members never interact with minors unsupervised. We work strictly in coordinated partnership with school leadership.

04. Secure Asset Stewardship

Raw footage, student names, and identification metadata are stored in encrypted, tier-access vaults with strict retention policies.
