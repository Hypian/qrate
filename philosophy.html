How we work

Check the facts, respect the people in the story and make things the client can keep using.

Start with what you can show

Before recording or designing, we ask what happened, who can speak about it and which evidence can be shared.

Four decisions that shape the work

Accuracy first

Check names, dates and figures with the people who own them.

Dignity is the brief

Let people choose what to share. Never ask someone to recreate distress for the camera.

Evidence over hype

Give the source and period behind a result. Leave out claims you cannot support.

Make work the client can reuse

Agree which files, templates and instructions the client needs at handover.

Filming and consent

Explain how the material will be used

Discuss permission with the partner before filming. Explain who will see the work and give participants a way to ask questions or raise concerns.

Plan with the school

Agree parent or guardian permission and student supervision with the school before filming children.

Keep the meaning intact

Check quotes and figures. When editing, keep the context that gives a person’s words their meaning.
